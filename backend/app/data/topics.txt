# News Anchor topic taxonomy (v2, Nov 2026).
# Backbone: the 17 top-level IPTC Media Topics (https://iptc.org/standards/media-topics/), the subject vocabulary news agencies use.
# Two levels only, and no topic names a place: "Indian markets" is Economy > Markets with the place India. Places come from the place axis.
# One topic per line: slug | Name | aliases | flags
#   Indentation (two spaces) makes a topic a sub-topic of the line above. The code is the slugs joined with a dot: sport.cricket.
#   aliases: comma-separated words or phrases matched in headlines and leads (whole words, any case).
#            An alias starting with ~ is ambiguous: it counts only with context (a related topic in the text, or a feed hint).
#   flags:   * = shown in menus by default. Others are collected and searchable too; they only stay out of the menus until chosen
#            in Settings > Topics. Categories are always shown.

# Phrases that only cover their words, so a shorter alias inside them does not count ("hacked to death" is a killing, not a hack).
